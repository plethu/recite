==================
Draft and malformed IDs remain recoverable
==================
:: recovery default
>
  A bare line header remains available while its label is being drafted.
> missing@
  A draft header without an anchor remains visible.
> malformed@bad
  A malformed anchor remains attached to its line.
? choice@NOT_AN_ANCHOR
  A malformed choice anchor remains attached to its choice.
  -> END
?
  A bare choice header remains available while its label is being drafted.
> long@0123456789abcdef01234
  An overlong hexadecimal suffix remains one malformed fragment.
> prefix_junk@0123456789abcdef0123junk
  A valid-looking prefix followed by junk remains one malformed fragment.
> valid@0123456789abcdef0123
  A valid anchor remains separately classified.
  -> END
